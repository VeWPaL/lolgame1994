CONTROLS

Depths is played with one hand on the keys and one on the mouse.

Keyboard

WASDMove. Arrow keys do the same.
QYour active item. One at a time — it lives in the middle plate under the map, and picking up a second one swaps it and drops the old one where you found the new one. Sigils are unlimited and have no key; those are the ones that change a run.
SHIFTSPACEBlink. Two charges, and you are untouchable for the whole jump. It recharges on its own; a cleared room tops it up.
ESCPPause.
RRestart, while paused or after a run.
HThis sheet.
BThe bug list: every fix in this build and why it is pinned.
SThis dungeon’s seed, on the title screen or from a pause. Type one to play exactly that dungeon again — the same rooms, the same bodies, the same weapons. Leave it empty for a new one.
FGFrame rate, and the room spawn plan.
TABYour character sheet: every stat, every sigil you are carrying, and what each one is doing. Press it again to close.

Mouse

Aim, and hold to cast whatever you are holding. Casting slows you down.
The right click. It is the Blast to begin with: it goes off on the first body it touches. The Hook lives in the secret room, and a second right click pulls it early.
Scrolling is not bound. A click on a pickup picks it up; a step onto a door goes through it.
A lunger that plants and draws a line is winding up a lunge. The line points at where you are going, not where you are: hold your heading and it lands, change it and it goes past you. Either way it cannot lunge twice in a row without a gap in between.
Escape or H closes this. The game does not take input while it is open. Clear a room for its key. The gold key opens the boss door.

CHARACTER

Depths on this seed, and what has been done to it.

Carried

Escape or P puts the game back down. It does not take input while this is open. Same seed, different build.

SEED

One short word decides the whole dungeon.

Empty gives you a new dungeon. A seed gives you exactly that one again — the same rooms, the same bodies in the same places, the same weapons in them.
